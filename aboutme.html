About Me

Hi! I'm Mimi Harrison, a recent physics, astronomy, and mathematics graduate of the Rochester Institute of Technology in Rochester, New York. I am originally from Dayton, Ohio, but I’m about to move to Tallahassee, Florida, to begin my physics Ph.D. at Florida State University. My research areas are early-universe galaxy formation and evolution, and theoretical astroparticle physics, with a focus on cosmic ray acceleration.


My interest in physics was born out of my love for nature and my desire to understand what confuses me. Outside of research work, I am still obsessed with the outdoors. I have been lucky enough to share my backyard with state wetlands and a plethora of creatures. A little over a year ago, I bought a cheap digital camera and began to document what I see on hikes, bike rides, and other adventures (All pictures displayed on this site were taken by and belong to me). I also collect four-leaf clover, feathers, and fortunes from fortune cookies.


I began creating this site in early June 2026 to experiment with a new medium for documenting my research work, summer reading list, and outdoor hobbies. Now, I am also using it to share my summer poetry final manuscript. I hope you enjoy!
