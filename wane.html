Wane

In the 5 o’clock sun Afternoon sours A bitter orange. Ginger vizslas, Sprawled across the Persian rug, Bathe in the warmth. All is still, but the Slow Thick Winding smoke Escaping his cigar. Dust suspended, Suddenly perturbed. Lazy spirals, Whirling unpredictably Through stale air. Dust, dander, and ashes, Fill grooves of The parquet floor. Dust that slowly falls In the cup of dark, Bitter, evening coffee. Stained old mug, How you balance Precariously atop A dusty mound of papers. In the seafoam sun-bleached sink, I wash away, That pungent taste Of late afternoon.
