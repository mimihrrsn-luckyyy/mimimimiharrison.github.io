Norman Fucking Rockwell! playing softly from the car radio

Everything looked faded in the most beautiful way — the docks, the trees, even the denim jackets hanging over the fence posts. The lake reflected the clouds almost perfectly tonight.

We stayed until after sunset reading quietly near the shoreline. Someone nearby was burning cedar wood and the smoke mixed with the humidity in the air.

The whole evening felt strangely preserved, like something already becoming a memory while it was happening.

“Summer’s meant for falling in love with places you know you’ll miss later.”
