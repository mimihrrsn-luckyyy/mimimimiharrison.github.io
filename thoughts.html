JULY 23, 2026
CEDAR LAKE
WEATHER: SUNNY
READING: THE VIRGIN SUICIDES

Late July

We arrived at the lake much later than expected. The water was perfectly still and reflected every cloud. It looked less like a landscape and more like a painting.

Most of the evening was spent reading quietly by the dock. The air smelled faintly of cedar and sunscreen. Every surface seemed to glow after sunset.

The entire night already felt nostalgic while it was happening.

AUGUST 2, 2026
NORTH SHORE
WEATHER: HUMID
SONG: MARINERS APARTMENT COMPLEX

Golden Hour

The light tonight turned everything honey-colored. Even the gravel road looked beautiful.

We stayed until after sunset and watched sailboats disappear into the haze.

It felt like the kind of memory that would become important years later.
